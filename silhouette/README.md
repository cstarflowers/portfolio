## What does Silhouette look like?
* [Watch the sample level HERE!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZ_4s9NMEwM)
  
In the following screenshots and gameplay video, you can see the unique DDR-style gameplay mechanics created in Silhouette. In order to do so, we converted the tracks in our project into 4-track MIDI files, with each track corresponding to an arrow direction. When the project plays the MIDI file, the notes will then spawn at their corresponding timestamp based on their track. As they approach the circle, users must tap the corresponding arrow key on their keyboard. Tapping too early or late will reset their combo and thus their point multiplier; tapping while the note is in the circle will increase their combo and points using the formula (100 * combo [max. 100]). They will only win and proceed to the next enemy if their total score passes the calculated score at the top of the level.
