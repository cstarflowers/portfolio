## What does Corrupted look like?
View the combat demo [here](https://youtu.be/n6KVC-JfGtk)

In these images and videos, you'll see the retro-style combat and gameplay mechanics of Corrupted. In terms of combat, this project is most closely related to Undertale, where each enemy will have a custom attack (or even a set of attacks, as seen in the video above), and the user will need to avoid their spawned projectiles. However, when the enemy spawns a golden projectile, users are encouraged to track it down and interact, as it'll damage the enemy and can increase their overall HP, allowing them to win the battle with ease.
